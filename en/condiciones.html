Prior Preparation and Recommendations for the Gentleman

Prior Hygiene

Arrive bathed for the appointment using warm water. This opens the hair follicles, relaxes the muscles, and guarantees an impeccable environment during the session.

Promotes extraction
no_photography Step 02

No Creams or Alcohol

Do not apply greasy creams, body oils, or alcohol-based deodorants to the areas to be waxed for 6 hours beforehand, to allow optimal adhesion of the wax.

Prevents hair slippage
straighten Step 03

Optimal Length: 3-5 mm

For waxing, the hair must be at least 3 to 5 millimeters long (approximately 2 weeks without shaving with a razor or close clipper).

Root extraction without breakage
dry_cleaning Step 04

Loose Clothing

Attend with loose and fresh cotton garments. Avoid synthetic clothing or tight lycra to mitigate any post-treatment friction in intimate areas or torso.

Soothing care of the dermis
wb_sunny

Aftercare (Post-waxing):

  • 1. First 24 hours: Avoid wet saunas, high-sweat exercise, and direct sun exposure.
  • 2. Pool or sea: Do not go to the pool or sea for three days (72h) after waxing, especially if done with wax.
  • 3. Exfoliation: After 2 or 3 weeks, or when hair starts growing back, we recommend gently exfoliating the waxed areas (especially with wax) to prevent folliculitis and ingrown hairs.
