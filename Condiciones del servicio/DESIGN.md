## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a private, high-end sanctuary aesthetic tailored exclusively for men's body grooming, waxing, and therapeutic wellness treatments. It departs completely from typical clinical med-spas and budget barber shops, adopting instead an architectural, private members' club atmosphere: quiet, masculine, discreet, and uncompromisingly luxurious.

### Visual Mood & Movement
The design blends **Tactile Minimalism** with **Subtle Dark Glassmorphism**:
- Deep warm obsidian surfaces (`#0F1115`) provide an intimate, confidential backdrop.
- Refined champagne gold touches reflect heritage quality, master craftsmanship, and top-tier hygienic care without visual gaudiness.
- Accents of forest emerald inject organic balance, nature-derived botanical care, and tranquility.
- Subtle frosted glass layers layered over dark substrates create depth and privacy, mirroring frosted privacy screens within luxury treatment suites.

## Colors

The palette is tuned for a dark-first luxury sensory experience:

- **Primary (`#D4AF37` - Champagne Gold):** Applied selectively to primary calls to action, badges of accreditation, focused states, and key monetary/service highlights. It brings warmth against dark environments without high-strain glare.
- **Secondary (`#1C3B2B` - Deep Forest Emerald):** Evokes natural botanicals, soothing post-treatment skin recovery, and classic masculine clubs.
- **Tertiary (`#9E7B3B` - Warm Antique Bronze):** Supports the primary gold for borders, delicate outlines, and muted metadata.
- **Neutral (`#0F1115` - Deep Warm Obsidian):** Serves as the primary canvas color. Elevated surface layers step up gradually to `#17191E` and `#22252B` to construct spatial depth.
- **Ivory Accent (`#FAF8F5`):** Reserved for primary typography, delivering crisp legibility with gentle warmth that avoids harsh pure-white luminescence.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy prioritizes restraint, architectural confidence, and effortless readability:

- **Display & Headings (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Modern geometric forms paired with high-end proportions. Headings feature subtle negative tracking to preserve weight and precision across hero spreads and treatment category showcases.
- **Body & Captions (Inter):** High-clarity neutral grotesque engineered for maximum legibility on dark surfaces, specifically when reviewing technical procedure details, contraindications, and pricing schedules.
- **Eyebrows & Micro-Labels:** Formatted in uppercase with generous tracking (`0.08em`) to subtly invoke bespoke perfumery and private grooming salon nomenclature.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column responsive grid underpins desktop templates (max content constraint: 1280px), shifting to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile. 

Generous negative space is vital: it communicates discretion, unhurried time, and personal attention. Section padding utilizes an amplified rhythmic scale:
- **Canvas Margins:** 3rem desktop margins contract to 1.25rem on mobile to preserve layout real estate for treatment selectors and schedules.
- **Section Breaks:** Generous vertical cushions (typically 5rem to 7rem on desktop) establish clear pauses between service categories (e.g., Brazilian/Intimate Waxing, Torso & Back, Body Massages).
- **Component Breathing Room:** Cards and modals enforce interior padding (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) to avoid dense, clinical packing.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical material staging rather than harsh drop shadows:

1. **Surface Tiers:**
   - **Canvas Level 0 (`#0F1115`):** Foundational backplate.
   - **Container Level 1 (`#17191E`):** Standard treatment cards, navigation headers, and modal backdrops.
   - **Floating Level 2 (`#22252B`):** Active drawers, selected treatment bundles, and popovers.

2. **Ambient Illumination:**
   Shadows are deeply diffused and tinted with dark obsidian tones: `0 16px 36px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)`. 

3. **Subtle Glassmorphism:**
   Sticky headers and floating action bars use `rgba(15, 17, 21, 0.75)` backdrop blur (`16px blur`) edged with a hairline golden-amber border (`1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.14)`).

4. **Hairline Gold Tracing:**
   Interactive panels and cards are framed by a 1px border (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)`). When hovered or focused, this stroke shifts to a refined radiant champagne gold (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.40)`) with a subtle `0 0 16px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.10)` glow.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Soft (Level 1)** geometric standard. Corner radii remain controlled (4px base up to 12px for prominent cards), projecting tailored precision, solid structure, and architectural masculinity.

- **Buttons & Small Interactive Targets:** 4px to 6px border radius.
- **Cards, Modals & Treatment Tiles:** 8px to 12px border radius.
- **Chips & Status Badges:** Soft capsules (pill-shaped) to distinguish quick-select body zones (e.g., *Pecho*, *Espalda*, *Zona Íntima*).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Reservar Cita):** Metallic champagne gradient backdrop (`linear-gradient(135deg, #DFBD69 0%, #D4AF37 50%, #C5A059 100%)`), dark obsidian label (`#0F1115`, `font-weight: 600`), 6px border radius, subtle warm highlight border.
- **Secondary Action (Consultar por WhatsApp / Detalles):** Ghost surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`), 1px stroke (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`), text in `#FAF8F5`. On hover, the border strengthens to `#D4AF37` with an ambient glow.

### Cards & Service Tiles
- Constructed using `#17191E` with a 1px border (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)`).
- Include distinct micro-badges for treatment durations (e.g., *45 min*, *Sesión Completa*), pricing rendered in high-contrast gold, and discrete privacy guarantees.
- Hover states smoothly elevate the card by `-2px` alongside an intensified gold border stroke.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background: `#111317` with subtle inner inset shadow.
- Border: 1px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#D4AF37`, accompanied by an ambient gold blur (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`).

### Service Selector Chips
- Inactive: `#17191E` with muted text (`#A1A5B0`) and subtle dark grey borders.
- Active/Selected: Rich forest emerald tint (`#1C3B2B`) or deep gold trim, paired with an ivory check indicator.

### Floating Booking Bar
- Pinned bottom bar for mobile appointment scheduling: `rgba(15, 17, 21, 0.88)` blur glass with single direct-touch WhatsApp and online booking triggers.
